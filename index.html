no such thing as a silly question 💕

let's talk about it, openly

does it hurt to insert? 🥺
it shouldn't! with a good fold and relaxed muscles, most girls feel light pressure at most. a drop of water or water-based lube on the rim helps a lot. give yourself 2–3 gentle cycles to become a total pro — everyone starts somewhere 💕
is it actually safe? 🛡️
yes! the fleur cup is medical-grade silicone — no BPA, no latex, no dyes. empty it every 8–10 hours, rinse well, wash your hands before handling, and boil between cycles. TSS with cups is extremely rare and almost always linked to wearing too long — stick to the 10-hour rule and you're golden.
how do i clean it in a public toilet? 🚽
easy: empty into the toilet, wipe with tissue (or rinse with a water bottle in the stall), reinsert. no sink in the stall needed. wash properly when you're home.
can teens & first-timers use it? 🌱
absolutely — size S was made for exactly that. start on a light day, take it slow, use the punch-down fold. patience > force, always. if you have an IUD or any pelvic health concerns, check with your doctor first 💜
can i sleep & swim with it? 🏊‍♀️
yes and YES. the gentle seal holds through sleep, swimming, yoga, dancing at weddings — all of it. this is honestly the best part.
how long does one cup last? ♻️
with good care, up to 5+ years. if it ever gets sticky, cracked or develops an odour that won't wash out, it's time for a new bloom.
